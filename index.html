StickerHub has retired.

We built StickerHub so you could share stickers without installing another app. You picked one on the website, messaged our bot, and it sent the sticker right back. Send the bot a sticker of your own, and it showed up on the website for everyone.

Thanks for every sticker you sent our way. 💚

How it worked

  1. Find a sticker

    Browse the site by category or tag, then tap the one you want.

  2. Message the bot

    The site opened WhatsApp or Telegram with the message already written. You just hit send.

  3. Get it back as a sticker

    The bot replied with the sticker itself, ready to save and forward.

  4. Or give one

    Sent the bot a sticker, and it uploaded it to the site. You got a link to name and tag it.

    Under the hood

    Website

    Vue 2 and Vuetify, hosted on Netlify. Pages for browsing, tagging and categorizing stickers.

    Chatbots

    Node.js. The WhatsApp bot drove WhatsApp Web with venom-bot; the Telegram bot used the Bot API.

    Storage

    Firebase: sticker files in Cloud Storage, names, tags and categories in Firestore.

    The bots are offline and this page is a read-only archive. WhatsApp bots now go through Meta's Business Platform, which needs a verified business number, so a hobby bot like ours has no easy way back.
